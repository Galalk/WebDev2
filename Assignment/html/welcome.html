TradeIt.com was founded in Ireland in 2001. It started as a personal college project that attracted a handfull of users who could post their old games they didnt want and trade with people anywhere in the world. It quickly blew up and had thousands of users in the first year and continued to grow until today where millions of users are buying, selling or trading games.
