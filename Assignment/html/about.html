About my project

My project is a website called TradeIt.com, which is a platform for buying, selling, and trading old video games. The website allows users to create accounts, list their games for sale or trade, and browse through a wide selection of second-hand games from other users. The goal of the project is to provide a convenient and user-friendly platform for gamers to exchange their old games with others in the gaming community.
